Everything your screenshots were missing

Five things SHOTO does the moment a screenshot lands in your library.

Swaps private details out

A card number becomes a different card number — same length, same place, and it still passes the same checks. A black bar announces there was something to hide. A replacement announces nothing.

Save in one tap

Share a screenshot from any app straight into SHOTO and pick its folder right there. You never have to open the app.

Search inside images

SHOTO reads the text in every screenshot and recognises what's pictured. Type “dog” and find the dog — even with no text at all.

Clears duplicates

Finds the near-identical copies cluttering your library and suggests which to keep. Nothing is ever deleted without you.

Merges long shots

Captured a long chat or article in pieces? SHOTO stitches them back into one clean, continuous image.

Your screenshots never leave your phone

Not a promise in a policy — it's how SHOTO is built. There's no cloud and no server, so there's nowhere else for your screenshots to go.

  • No cloud storage, no servers, no uploads
  • Search and recognition run offline, on your device
  • No account needed — the library belongs to the phone
